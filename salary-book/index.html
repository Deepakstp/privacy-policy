Privacy Policy — Salary Book

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

App: Salary Book (com.diagnolite.salarybook)

Developer: Deepak Kumar (Diagnolite)

Core Privacy Promise: Salary Book is an offline-first employee wage, attendance, and overtime tracking application. Your financial data, attendance dates, and salary figures belong strictly to you and remain stored locally on your device.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

We do not operate remote servers to collect, upload, or sell your personal records. All data you input is handled strictly as follows:

  • Local Database (SQLite): All attendance records, shift timings, overtime hours, advances, and profile details are stored locally inside the app's secure private storage on your phone.
  • Device-Bound: If you uninstall the app or clear app data via Android Settings without creating a manual backup, all local records are permanently wiped.

2. App Permissions & Sensitive Features

Salary Book requests only the bare minimum permissions necessary for user-requested operations:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to send optional daily attendance logging reminders and salary pay-day alerts scheduled by you.
  • Biometric / Fingerprint Authentication: Used exclusively to unlock the app locally via Android's native BiometricPrompt API. Your biometric data is encrypted by your device hardware and is never accessible to the app.
  • Location Notice: Salary Book does not track, collect, or store your GPS location. No location permissions are requested.

3. Cloud Backup (Optional & User-Initiated)

If you choose to use the Google Drive Cloud Backup feature:

  • The app communicates directly with official Google Drive APIs via OAuth 2.0 authentication.
  • Backup files are stored exclusively inside your private Google Drive AppData folder, isolated from any external access.
  • Backups only run when manually triggered or when auto-backup is explicitly enabled by you.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Salary Book utilizes Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive advertisements:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers such as Android Advertising ID (AAID) and basic device telemetry to serve advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.
  • User Messaging Platform (UMP): In eligible regions (e.g., EEA/UK), users are presented with a consent dialog to manage personalized vs. non-personalized ad preferences.
  • Data Segregation: Your salary calculations, attendance dates, and notes are never shared with advertisers or third-party networks.

5. Children's Privacy

Salary Book is intended for working professionals and individuals managing payroll or attendance. It is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
