Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

App Name: Salary Book

Package Name: com.diagnolite.salarybook

Developer: Deepak Kumar (Diagnolite)

Core Privacy Commitment: Salary Book is designed with an offline-first philosophy. Your financial data, attendance records, overtime hours, and salary calculations belong exclusively to you and remain strictly on your device.

1. Data Storage & Management

Salary Book does not upload your sensitive financial details to private servers:

  • Local SQLite Database: All daily attendance entries (Present, Absent, Half-Day, Leaves), overtime hours, advances, and salary figures are stored directly in your phone's isolated, secure application storage.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal phone numbers to use the app.
  • Data Deletion: If you uninstall the app or clear app data via device settings, all records stored locally are permanently erased.

2. Permissions & Security

We believe in absolute transparency and least-privilege permissions:

  • Biometric Lock (Fingerprint / Face): Used strictly on-device via Android BiometricPrompt to protect your payroll records from unauthorized local access. Biometric data is never read or stored by Salary Book.
  • Notifications: Used only to deliver daily attendance reminders, overtime alerts, or monthly salary pay-day reminders scheduled by you.
  • Zero Location Tracking: Salary Book does not track, request, or store your GPS location.

3. Cloud Backup (Optional)

If you choose to use the Google Drive Cloud Backup feature:

  • The app communicates directly with official Google Drive APIs via secure OAuth 2.0.
  • Backup files are stored inside your private Google Drive AppData folder, inaccessible to other apps.
  • Backups are only performed when you manually trigger them or enable auto-backup.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to support the continued development of this free application. AdMob utilizes standard advertising identifiers to serve ads:

  • Data Collection by AdMob: AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • User Consent (UMP SDK): In regions requiring consent (such as the EEA and UK), we integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to allow users to manage their ad consent choices.
  • Data Segregation: Your salary calculations, attendance status, and financial ledger entries are completely segregated and never shared with Google AdMob or any advertising network.

5. Children's Privacy

Salary Book is intended for working individuals and employees managing payroll. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.
